## OUR JOURNEY & MISSION

Established in China in 2017, Alchemy Media is deeply committed to fostering the growth of independent artists and record labels. Our core objective is to bridge the gap between raw creative talent and sustainable commercial success, transforming emerging musical voices into dynamic, impactful brands. By seamlessly integrating deep cultural insights with data-driven marketing strategies, we deliver the essential infrastructure and support that independent creators need to break through the noise and thrive in today's global music ecosystem.


## OUR SERVICES & SOLUTIONS

### 🌐 Cross-Border Promotion & Global Bridging

Erasing borders to connect independent music with global audiences.

The modern music landscape is completely global, but navigating different cultural ecosystems requires a specialist. Alchemy Media serves as a definitive cross-border bridge.

Globalizing Chinese Music: We empower Chinese independent artists and labels to scale overseas. We navigate the international landscape to secure placements on global platforms like Spotify, Apple Music, and YouTube, while orchestrating Western PR campaigns, playlist pitching, and localized social media strategies.

Importing Global Talent to China: We help overseas artists and record labels break into the highly dynamic Chinese music market. Our team handles the localized complexities of executing promotional campaigns across mainland ecosystems, including NetEase Cloud Music, Tencent Music (QQ Music), Douyin, and Xiaohongshu (RED), translating international buzz into a dedicated regional fanbase.

### 📦 Digital Music Global Distribution

Seamlessly deliver your music to the world while retaining full ownership.

We ensure your sound is available on every major global and regional streaming platform—including Spotify, Apple Music, Tencent Music, and NetEase Cloud Music. Beyond basic delivery, Alchemy Media handles the complex backend infrastructure. We optimize your metadata, streamline your publishing setup, and tap into global collection networks to ensure every cent of your mechanical and streaming royalties finds its way back to you.

### 💿 Physical Record Distribution & Retail

Bringing independent music into the physical world.

In a digital-first world, tangible music assets forge the deepest connections with superfans. Alchemy Media operates a robust physical distribution network to get your music onto store shelves worldwide. We handle the end-to-end logistics of distributing Vinyl, CDs, and limited-edition cassettes to independent record shops, specialized music boutiques, and major global retail chains. From manufacturing coordination and customs clearance to inventory management and retail placement, we ensure your physical releases are highly accessible to collectors and physical audiophiles alike.

### 👕 Creative Merchandise Manufacturing

Transforming your brand identity into high-quality, wearable culture.

For independent artists and labels, merchandise is more than just a revenue stream—it is a vital badge of community identity. Alchemy Media handles the end-to-end logistics of merchandise production. We partner with reliable, premium manufacturers to produce everything from standard apparel and accessories to complex, bespoke collector's items. By managing product sourcing, quality control, cost optimization, and manufacturing deadlines, we ensure your fans receive retail-ready merchandise that looks exceptional and lasts.

### 🎨 Art Direction & Visual Design

Giving your sound a definitive, unforgettable visual identity.

In a crowded digital space, striking visuals are what capture a listener's attention before they ever hit play. Our creative division delivers comprehensive art direction and visual design tailored strictly to your music's subculture. We oversee and execute everything a modern music brand requires—including eye-catching album artwork, single covers, promotional tour posters, digital motion graphics, and cohesive brand identity kits for labels. We translate audio concepts into powerful visual assets that resonate across social media, streaming platforms, and physical media alike.

### 📅 Live Booking & Touring

Transition from the studio to the stage.

Connecting artists with live audiences is where real community is forged. Alchemy Media bridges the gap between independent talent and live music venues, festivals, and promoters. We manage the heavy lifting of live execution—from routing regional and international tours to negotiating performance contracts and securing competitive guarantees. We ensure our artists step onto the stage with optimal technical, logistical, and financial support.

### 💼 Career & Label Management

Professional infrastructure to scale your music business.

To transform independent music into a sustainable business, you need structure. We act as the operational backbone for emerging artists and independent record labels. Our management division oversees long-term career mapping, release scheduling, brand partnerships, and asset management. By handling the complex operational, financial, and legal logistics, we give creators the freedom to focus entirely on the art while we scale the brand.
